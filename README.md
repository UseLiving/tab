# Tab ($TAB)

**Your tab, finally itemized.** Paste a wallet and a thermal printer rings up the receipt the bar never gave you.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/tab/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseTabTrades

## What you can do
- Paste a wallet (or connect one, read-only) and watch your receipt print, then save it as a PNG or copy it to post
- Climb the Hall of Shame leaderboard, from LEFT THE BAR to THE BAR OWES YOU A PLAQUE
- Watch the refund desk: a round timer, a growing pot, and the line of biggest tabs
- Check your place in line, read the store policy and how each line is calculated

Single self-contained `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser.

> For laughs: receipts are simulated, generated from the address you paste, not read from the chain. Play money only, not financial advice.
